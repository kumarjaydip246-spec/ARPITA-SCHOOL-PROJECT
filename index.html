About Our School

OUR STORY

Divine Happy School began on January 21, 1991, in a single building with roughly 200 students. Its founders, Sri Mathura Kumar and Smt. Droupadi Devi, were not businesspeople entering education. They were teachers. Veteran educators who had spent their careers in classrooms and believed that Bhagalpur needed an institution built on a simple principle: that discipline, values, and rigorous academics could coexist, and that a school built on this foundation would outlast any trend. The neighbourhood around Chatrapati Talab Road was sparse in those days. The school didn't arrive in a thriving locality. Over the decades, the area developed around it. The school shaped its students, its teachers built careers within its walls, and the community around it grew as the institution's reputation drew families to the area. It is one of the rare cases where an institution didn't just serve a neighbourhood. It helped build one.

Acadmics

A school is only as strong as the teachers who show up every morning. At Divine Happy School, 70+ teachers walk into their classrooms each day with one job — to make sure every student leaves understanding more than they did when they walked in. The school's faculty brings deep classroom experience, with many having served for over a decade. They have watched batches grow from nervous first-graders to confident board examinees. They know which concepts trip students up year after year, where to slow down, where to push harder, and when a child needs encouragement more than instruction. That kind of understanding isn't trained. It's earned over years of being in the room. All teaching staff hold CBSE-mandated training certifications, with regular participation in workshops and professional development programmes. But what sets this faculty apart isn't paperwork. It's presence. The same teachers who deliver the lesson also mentor students through exams, guide them through co-curriculars, and build the kind of trust that keeps parents confident their child is in steady hands.

Campus & Facilities

The campus spans approximately 5 acres, organised across three academic blocks. Each one was purpose-built for a different stage of the school. 75+ classrooms across the campus, designed for natural light and ventilation. Smartboards are installed across senior classes and the early-years block, with expansion continuing across the campus. Every smartboard-equipped classroom runs on the same content that students access at home through eVidya, so what is taught in class is exactly what gets revised at home. Four dedicated science laboratories: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and a Composite Science lab for middle school. Each lab is separately equipped for the full range of CBSE practical work, from basic experiments in Class VI to the board-level practicals that count towards the final grade in Class X and XII. Three computer labs across Blocks A, B, and C, supporting the Computer Science curriculum from Class III onward. Students learn coding fundamentals, digital literacy, and applied computing as part of the regular timetable, not as an extracurricular. A collection of 5,000+ books, from picture books for the early years to reference material for board and competitive exam preparation. The library serves as a quiet reading and study space throughout the school day. For digital resources, students use eVidya at home, which complements the physical collection with chapter-wise learning material. A dedicated music room equipped for vocal and instrumental practice. Used for curriculum music classes, preparation for cultural programmes, and school events including Annual Day performances. A 150 to 200 seat auditorium with audio-visual equipment for school assemblies, performances, guest lectures, parent orientations, and ceremonies. This is the central gathering space for the school community.

Our aim is to create a friendly learning environment where every child can discover their potential.

Our Vision

The vision of school was conceptualized by Sri Mathura Kumar and Smt. Droupadi Devi, who were renowned educationists through their lifetime. The vision was brought to life by Former Director Dr. Gopal Krishna Kunwar who worked tirelessly to bring the dream to life by establishing the campus. We, at Divine Happy School, are committed to keep continue to live the vision and take it to new and unscaled heights. The school fosters students' academic and social potential, emphasizing practical learning over rote memorization to prepare them for future challenges. It guides pupils through diverse real-world situations, building resilience and an optimistic mindset to navigate life's ups and downs responsibly and respectfully.
